# Yok Health Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 10, 2026**

Yok Health respects your privacy and is designed to help you understand and track your health and fitness information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Yok Health accesses, what information may be stored, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Account Information

When you create a Yok Health account, we may collect and store:

- Email address
- User account identifier

This information is used to create and authenticate your account and to associate your saved information with your account.

Yok Health currently uses Supabase to provide authentication, database, and backend infrastructure.

## 2. Apple Health Data

With your permission, Yok Health may access selected information from Apple Health, including:

- Step count
- Sleep information
- Weight
- Resting heart rate
- Workout activity and workout duration

Yok Health requests access only to health information needed to provide features available within the app.

You control whether Yok Health can access each type of Apple Health information. You can change or revoke Apple Health permissions through Apple Health or the appropriate settings on your Apple device.

## 3. How Health Information Is Used

Health and fitness information may be used to provide features including:

- Health Dashboard
- Health Score
- Personal Baseline
- Health and fitness trends
- Progress toward personal goals
- Personalized health and activity summaries

Health Score, trends, summaries, and other information provided by Yok Health are intended for general informational and wellness purposes.

They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition and should not be considered medical advice.

## 4. Cloud Storage

Some information obtained from Apple Health may be securely synchronized with Yok Health's backend infrastructure to provide features such as:

- Daily health history
- Health trends
- Personal Baseline
- Account-based data synchronization

Information stored in the backend is associated with your authenticated Yok Health account.

## 5. Personal Health Goals

Yok Health allows you to create personal goals, including goals related to:

- Daily steps
- Sleep duration
- Weekly exercise duration

These goals may be stored with your Yok Health account so they can be restored when you sign in again or use the service on another supported device.

## 6. Advertising and Sale of Health Data

Yok Health does not sell your health information.

Yok Health does not use Apple Health information for advertising, marketing, or targeted advertising.

Yok Health does not provide your health information to advertising platforms or data brokers.

## 7. Data Access and Security

Yok Health is designed so authenticated users can access data associated with their own accounts.

Backend access controls are used to restrict access to account-associated information.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the service. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Account and Data Deletion

You can initiate permanent account deletion directly within Yok Health.

When account deletion is confirmed, Yok Health is designed to delete:

- Your Yok Health account
- Health information stored by Yok Health and associated with that account
- Personal health goals associated with that account

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Deleting your Yok Health account does not delete information stored independently by Apple Health on your Apple device.

## 9. Apple Health Permissions

Yok Health cannot access Apple Health information unless you grant the applicable permission.

You may change or revoke those permissions independently of deleting your Yok Health account.

## 10. Data Retention

Account-associated information is retained while necessary to provide Yok Health and maintain your account.

When you permanently delete your account, account-associated information that Yok Health is responsible for retaining will be deleted, except where retention is required by applicable law or necessary for legitimate legal obligations.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Yok Health is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental or guardian consent is legally required.

Age eligibility and any additional protections required before public release will be implemented according to the markets in which Yok Health is made available.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Yok Health features, data practices, legal requirements, or service providers change.

The latest version will identify its effective or last-updated date.

## 13. Contact

For questions, privacy requests, or concerns regarding Yok Health or this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the official Yok Health support contact information.

A dedicated public contact address will be provided before Yok Health is released on the App Store.
