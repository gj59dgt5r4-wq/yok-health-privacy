# Yok Health Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Yok Health respects your privacy and is designed to help you understand your personal health and wellness data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Yok Health may access, how that information is used, where it may be stored, and the choices you have regarding your data.

## 1. Account Information

Yok Health may collect and store account information required to provide app features.

This may include:

- Email address
- Account identifier
- Authentication information managed through our authentication provider

Your email address is used for account access, authentication, account recovery, and support-related purposes.

## 2. Apple Health Data

With your permission, Yok Health may read selected information from Apple Health.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

- Step count
- Body weight
- Resting heart rate
- Sleep information
- Workout activity
- Workout duration

Yok Health requests access only to health and fitness information needed for app features.

You control Apple Health permissions and may change or revoke access at any time through the Health app or iPhone Settings.

## 3. How Health Data Is Used

Health and fitness information may be used to:

- Display your current health and fitness information
- Track personal trends over time
- Calculate wellness-oriented progress indicators
- Compare progress with your personal health goals
- Create health and fitness summaries
- Support personalized wellness recommendations
- Help identify changes in activity, sleep, workouts, and other supported metrics

Yok Health is intended for general health, fitness, and wellness purposes.

It is not intended to provide medical diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, or emergency medical services.

## 4. Cloud Data Storage

Some Yok Health information may be securely transmitted from your device and stored in cloud infrastructure used by Yok Health.

Current cloud storage may include:

- Account information
- Daily step summaries
- Body weight
- Resting heart rate
- Sleep summaries
- Workout summaries
- Personal health goals

Yok Health currently uses Supabase for authentication, database, and related backend services.

Data stored in Yok Health accounts is associated with the authenticated user so that each user can access only their own information through the app.

## 5. Personal Health Goals

Yok Health allows you to create and store personal goals, including goals related to:

- Daily steps
- Sleep duration
- Weekly workout time

These goals may be used to personalize progress indicators and wellness summaries.

## 6. Advertising and Sale of Data

Yok Health does not use your health data for advertising.

Yok Health does not sell your personal information or health information.

Yok Health does not use Apple Health data for targeted advertising, marketing data mining, or cross-app tracking.

## 7. Data Linked to Your Account

Information stored in Yok Health cloud services may be linked to your Yok Health account so that the app can provide your personal history, goals, and summaries.

This may include your email address, health summaries, fitness summaries, and health goals.

## 8. Data Accuracy

Health and fitness information may originate from Apple Health, Apple Watch, connected devices, manual entries, or other supported sources.

Some information may be incomplete, delayed, estimated, or inaccurate.

Yok Health should not be used as the sole basis for medical decisions.

## 9. Security

Yok Health uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect user information.

These measures may include:

- Authenticated user accounts
- Secure network connections
- Database access controls
- Row-level security
- Server-side handling of privileged credentials

No system can guarantee absolute security, but Yok Health is designed to minimize unnecessary access to personal and health information.

## 10. Account Deletion

You can initiate permanent account deletion directly from the Yok Health app.

The account deletion option is available in Settings.

When account deletion is completed, Yok Health is designed to delete:

- Your Yok Health authentication account
- Health summaries associated with your account
- Personal health goals associated with your account
- Other Yok Health cloud information associated with the deleted account where applicable

Deleted accounts and associated data may not be recoverable.

Information may be retained only when required by applicable law or necessary for legitimate security or legal obligations.

## 11. Apple Health Permissions

You may revoke Yok Health access to Apple Health information at any time.

You can manage these permissions through:

- The Apple Health app
- iPhone Settings

If you revoke access, some Yok Health features may no longer have enough information to provide certain summaries, trends, or progress indicators.

## 12. Data Retention

Yok Health retains account-related information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service or until the associated account is deleted, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

## 13. Children's Privacy

Yok Health is not designed to knowingly collect personal health information from children in circumstances where parental consent or other legal authorization is required.

## 14. Third-Party Services

Yok Health may rely on third-party infrastructure providers to operate app features.

Current services may include:

- Apple Health
- Supabase

These services may process information according to their own privacy and security practices.

## 15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Yok Health changes, new features are added, or legal and operational requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page.
