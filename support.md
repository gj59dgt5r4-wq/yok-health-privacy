# Yok Health Support

Welcome to Yok Health Support.

Yok Health is a personal health and wellness app designed to help you understand trends from Apple Health data, track personal goals, and review your progress over time.

## Getting Help

If you experience a problem with Yok Health, please include the following information when contacting support:

- A short description of the problem
- What you were trying to do
- Your iPhone model and iOS version
- The Yok Health app version
- Any error message shown in the app

Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, or other sensitive account credentials.

## Apple Health Permissions

Yok Health may request permission to read selected Apple Health data, such as:

- Step count
- Body weight
- Resting heart rate
- Sleep
- Workout activity

You can change these permissions at any time from the Health app or iPhone Settings.

If access is disabled, some Yok Health features may not have enough data to calculate trends or summaries.

## Account and Data

Yok Health uses an account to keep your health goals and synced health summaries associated with the correct user.

You can sign out from the Settings screen.

You can also request permanent account deletion directly from inside the app. Account deletion is designed to remove the account and the related Yok Health cloud data.

## Privacy

Yok Health does not use your health data for advertising.

Yok Health does not sell your personal or health data.

For more information, please read the Yok Health Privacy Policy.

## Health Information Disclaimer

Yok Health is intended for general health, fitness, and wellness tracking.

It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical services.

For medical concerns, symptoms, or treatment decisions, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

A dedicated Yok Health support contact address will be provided before the app is publicly released on the App Store.
