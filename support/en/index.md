# YOK Health Help Center

## Getting help and contact

For help, questions, or assistance with YOK Health, email [yokhealth.app@gmail.com](mailto:yokhealth.app@gmail.com). Do not include passwords, authentication codes, or unnecessary health information in your message.

## App usage

We can help explain how to use health tracking, nutrition logging, goals, settings, and other features available in the app. Please describe the screen and action you need help with.

## Technical issues

If the app is not working as expected, include your device model, iOS version, YOK Health app version, and the steps that led to the issue. Screenshots are helpful when they do not expose private information.

## Account and data

For questions about signing in, signing out, profile settings, synchronization, or your stored data, contact support from the email address associated with your account when practical. Never send your password or verification codes.

## Apple Health permissions

YOK Health may request access to selected Apple Health data. You can review or revoke access at any time in the Health app or iPhone Settings. If expected data is missing, confirm that the relevant permission is enabled and that the data exists in Apple Health.

## Privacy questions

For questions about how YOK Health handles personal or health information, contact support or review the Privacy Policy. YOK Health does not sell personal or health information and does not use health data for advertising.

## Account deletion

You can start permanent account deletion from YOK Health Settings. Deletion is irreversible after it completes. If you cannot access the in-app deletion control, contact support for assistance.

## Feedback and issue reporting

Suggestions and issue reports are welcome at [yokhealth.app@gmail.com](mailto:yokhealth.app@gmail.com). Explain what you expected, what happened, and how to reproduce it. Avoid sending sensitive information unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

YOK Health supports general health, fitness, and wellness tracking. It is not an emergency service and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. For urgent medical concerns, contact an appropriate healthcare professional or local emergency service.
